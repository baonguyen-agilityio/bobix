Test build of Bobix 0.2.0. Bobix is now a personal assistant for working well with Claude Code: it shows who is working and who needs you, what each session costs, and which skills you can drop. This is a pre-release; the newest stable version is still v0.1.3.

## What's new

- **New flat-icon pets.** The pixel creatures are gone. Five flat pets (the Bobix creature, Nota the notebook, Tty the terminal, Miso the cat, Ship the rocket) walk, type, wait, wave and jump, with a status badge. A project keeps one pet; each session has its own colour. They show on screen and in the panel.
- **A new panel, built like a launcher.** One window: a search field, then Overview, Sessions, Work, Skills, Room and Settings, the list on the left and the details on the right. Work and Room are real screens now (see below). ↑ ↓ move, ↵ does the main action, ⌘K shows every action. It follows your light or dark theme.
- **Approve from the pet or the panel.** When Claude asks for permission, answer on the pet, in the panel or on the notification (macOS). Claude still asks in the terminal at the same time, so you can answer in either place.
- **Usage per session, with warnings.** Tokens, models, active time and the part spent re-reading context, against your own usual session. Dollars are an API-equivalent estimate you can hide. Three warnings say why and what to do: a long session, a heavy model on light work, read-only exploration on a heavy model. Thresholds are in Settings.
- **Skills audit.** For a project: every skill Claude loads, how often each was used in the last 30 days, unused skills and duplicate sets, and what the descriptions cost per turn. Disable or remove your own skills; every change keeps a backup and has Undo. Plugin skills are not changed.
- **Approval count on the app icon.** The number of sessions waiting for your approval shows next to the menu bar icon (macOS, `9+` above 9), on the Dock while the panel is open, and as a coral pill on the tray icon (Windows, Linux). Settings → Notifications → "Count on the app icon" turns it off; Do not disturb hides it.
- **Habits become personal skills.** Skills → "From your habits". Once a week, only if you turned on both "Read my prompts" and the weekly scan, your own `claude -p` finds requests you repeat in two weeks running. You get a draft skill to edit and approve; only Approve writes `~/.claude/skills/<name>/SKILL.md`. Every write is a version with Undo, and a skill Bobix made is improved only on evidence of use. Nothing is ever written into a project, and no prompt is kept.
- **Ticket flow (GitLab).** Work screen: Start a ticket in its own git worktree, the session plans first, you approve the plan once and it runs on in auto mode for that session only. A work log keeps the steps and the assumptions; a new assumption puts an amber "?" on the pet. Clean up the worktree when done (the branch and the log stay).
- **War room over your company Tailscale.** Room screen: one room per problem, with chat, the host's Claude arranging the chat into problem, options, pros and cons and a lean, and an AI decision record both sides keep. Invites go by Tailscale login; the room runs only on the host's Tailscale address while it is open. There is an audit log, and common secret shapes are redacted from chat (not every secret).
- **Fixes since the first notes.** Habit cards and stored habits are back in Overview and Settings; a ticket's plan reads "Approve the plan"; the Room audit log shows logins; a blocked flow no longer looks green; "1 approval" reads right; Room search reads clearly.
- **Model rules for CLAUDE.md.** A short "when to split, which model" block. Bobix shows the exact text and adds it only after you agree; Undo restores the file as it was.
- **New app icon.** The Bobix creature as a flat pet on a navy tile, for the app, the installers and the site; the menu bar icon follows the bar's light or dark look on macOS.
- **New site and docs.** The landing page (English and Vietnamese), the READMEs with new screenshots and demo, and the roadmap describe the new product.

## What to test

Install over rc.4 (macOS first), then:

- [ ] **Upgrade.** Open rc.5 over rc.4: your settings, project colours and hooks are still there, and the hooks do not need reinstalling.
- [ ] **New pets.** Start a few sessions. Check that a pet walks while Claude works, waits with a ring and "!" when it needs you, waves and jumps when done, and sleeps when idle. Two sessions of one project share a pet in different colours.
- [ ] **Panel.** Open it from the tray or menu bar. Search, move with ↑ ↓, press ⌘K, switch screens with ⌘1 to ⌘6, try a narrow window and both themes.
- [ ] **Approvals.** Make Claude ask for a permission. Allow and Deny from the pet, then from the panel (↵), then from the notification. Check the terminal prompt also still works.
- [ ] **Usage and warnings.** Open Overview during a real session: tokens and "x times your usual session" appear. Lower a threshold in Settings to see a warning with its reason. Hide the dollar figure.
- [ ] **Skills audit with undo.** Skills screen, pick a project. Disable one of your own skills, then Undo; remove one, then Undo. Check the folder is back as it was. Open a plugin skill: it must refuse and show the setting to change.
- [ ] **Model rules.** Add the block to a test project's `CLAUDE.md`, check the text, then Remove it: the file must be as it was.
- [ ] **Approval count.** Make two sessions wait for approval: the number shows on the menu bar icon (macOS) and the Dock while the panel is open. Answer one and it drops; turn on Do not disturb and it hides; turn off "Count on the app icon" in Settings → Notifications and it goes.
- [ ] **Habits to skills.** Turn on "Read my prompts" and the weekly scan, then Scan now. Check a habit has its evidence, make a draft, edit it, Approve (the file appears in `~/.claude/skills`), then Undo. Check that nothing is written into a project.
- [ ] **Ticket flow.** On a GitLab project with a ticket, Work → Start in a worktree. Check the worktree is made, the session plans first, and "Approve the plan" lets it run on. Check the work log fills in, a new assumption shows the "?" on the pet, and Clean up worktree removes the folder but keeps the branch.
- [ ] **War room.** Room → create a room, invite someone by Tailscale login, chat, make the draft, edit it and Decide. Check the guest sees the same record, a person who is not invited is refused and shown in the audit log, and a pasted secret is redacted.
- [ ] **Fixes.** Overview shows the habit cards and Settings shows what is kept with Delete all; a plan approval reads "Approve the plan"; one pending approval reads "1 approval".
- [ ] **Settings and Setup.** Setup and Feedback are now under Settings. Remove hooks and Install hooks still work.

## Known gaps

- Builds are not code-signed: macOS and Windows warn on first launch.
- Checked by eye on macOS only. Windows and Linux are covered by the CI smoke run, not by hand; usage reading on those systems assumes Claude writes transcripts where it does on macOS.
- The room was tested on one Mac only, not between two machines.
- macOS asks once to allow incoming connections when you host a room.
- The ticket flow was not run inside the Claude desktop app.
- The ticket flow works with GitLab only.
- The skills audit does not edit plugin settings, and Bobix does not yet suggest a model at Start on a ticket or in a pet tip. The first audit after start reads 30 days of transcripts and can take several seconds.
- The hourly usage chart from the design is left out; Overview shows the live sessions' token split instead.
- Picture pets (a pet drawn from your own picture) are hidden in this build.
