Test build of Bobix 0.2.0. Bobix is now a personal assistant for working well with Claude Code: it shows who is working and who needs you, what each session costs, and which skills you can drop. This is a pre-release; the newest stable version is still v0.1.3.

## What's new

- **New flat-icon pets.** The pixel creatures are gone. Five flat pets (the Bobix creature, Nota the notebook, Tty the terminal, Miso the cat, Ship the rocket) walk, type, wait, wave and jump, with a status badge. A project keeps one pet; each session has its own colour. They show on screen and in the panel.
- **A new panel, built like a launcher.** One window: a search field, then Overview, Sessions, Work, Skills, Room and Settings, the list on the left and the details on the right. ↑ ↓ move, ↵ does the main action, ⌘K shows every action. It follows your light or dark theme.
- **Approve from the pet or the panel.** When Claude asks for permission, answer on the pet, in the panel or on the notification (macOS). Claude still asks in the terminal at the same time, so you can answer in either place.
- **Usage per session, with warnings.** Tokens, models, active time and the part spent re-reading context, against your own usual session. Dollars are an API-equivalent estimate you can hide. Three warnings say why and what to do: a long session, a heavy model on light work, read-only exploration on a heavy model. Thresholds are in Settings.
- **Skills audit.** For a project: every skill Claude loads, how often each was used in the last 30 days, unused skills and duplicate sets, and what the descriptions cost per turn. Disable or remove your own skills; every change keeps a backup and has Undo. Plugin skills are not changed.
- **Model rules for CLAUDE.md.** A short "when to split, which model" block. Bobix shows the exact text and adds it only after you agree; Undo restores the file as it was.
- **New site and docs.** The landing page (English and Vietnamese) and the roadmap describe the new product.

## What to test

Install over rc.4 (macOS first), then:

- [ ] **Upgrade.** Open rc.5 over rc.4: your settings, project colours, pet pictures and hooks are still there, and the hooks do not need reinstalling.
- [ ] **New pets.** Start a few sessions. Check that a pet walks while Claude works, waits with a ring and "!" when it needs you, waves and jumps when done, and sleeps when idle. Two sessions of one project share a pet in different colours. A picture chosen in Change pet still works.
- [ ] **Panel.** Open it from the tray or menu bar. Search, move with ↑ ↓, press ⌘K, switch screens with ⌘1 to ⌘6, try a narrow window and both themes.
- [ ] **Approvals.** Make Claude ask for a permission. Allow and Deny from the pet, then from the panel (↵), then from the notification. Check the terminal prompt also still works.
- [ ] **Usage and warnings.** Open Overview during a real session: tokens and "x times your usual session" appear. Lower a threshold in Settings to see a warning with its reason. Hide the dollar figure.
- [ ] **Skills audit with undo.** Skills screen, pick a project. Disable one of your own skills, then Undo; remove one, then Undo. Check the folder is back as it was. Open a plugin skill: it must refuse and show the setting to change.
- [ ] **Model rules.** Add the block to a test project's `CLAUDE.md`, check the text, then Remove it: the file must be as it was.
- [ ] **Settings and Setup.** Setup and Feedback are now under Settings. Remove hooks and Install hooks still work.

## Known gaps

- Builds are not code-signed: macOS and Windows warn on first launch.
- Checked by eye on macOS only. Windows and Linux are covered by the CI smoke run, not by hand; usage reading on those systems assumes Claude writes transcripts where it does on macOS.
- Work and Room in the panel are placeholders (the ticket flow and the war room are next).
- The skills audit does not edit plugin settings, and Bobix does not yet suggest a model at Start on a ticket or in a pet tip. The first audit after start reads 30 days of transcripts and can take several seconds.
- The hourly usage chart from the design is left out; Overview shows the live sessions' token split instead.
- Pets with a picture you chose keep their pixel look. In "Show when needed" the pet under the balloon is a small pixel version.
- The demo video and GIF in the README still show the old pixel pets.
